Common Cyber Attacks

Phishing Attacks

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals send fake emails or messages to trick users into revealing passwords, OTPs, or bank details.

Malware Attacks

Malware is malicious software such as viruses, worms, and Trojans designed to damage or steal data from systems.

Ransomware Attacks

Ransomware locks or encrypts files and demands payment to restore access. It is one of the most dangerous cyber threats.

Identity Theft

Identity theft happens when attackers steal personal information like Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details to commit fraud.

Data Breach

A data breach occurs when hackers access confidential information from companies or organizations without permission.
