Cyber Fraud Prevention Tips

👤 For Individuals

  • Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs with anyone.
  • Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
  • Avoid clicking on suspicious links in emails or SMS.
  • Do not download apps from unknown sources.
  • Regularly update your mobile and computer software.
  • Check bank statements frequently for unauthorized transactions.

🏢 For Organizations

  • Conduct regular cybersecurity awareness training for employees.
  • Use firewalls, antivirus, and endpoint protection systems.
  • Implement strict access control and role-based permissions.
  • Back up critical data regularly and securely.
  • Monitor systems for unusual or suspicious activity.
  • Enforce strong password policies in the organization.
  • Conduct periodic security audits and penetration testing.

🚨 If You Become a Victim

  • Immediately contact your bank and freeze transactions.
  • Report the incident to cybercrime authorities.
  • Change all compromised passwords immediately.
  • Keep records of messages, emails, or calls received.
